# Hero photos (Section 01)

These are the first photos Glyde sees. Choose your favorites.

| File | Where it appears | Shape | Recommended size |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero-main.jpg` | Large arched frame beside the heading | Portrait 4:5 | 1200 × 1500 px |
| `hero-secondary.jpg` | Polaroid overlapping the main photo | Square 1:1 | 900 × 900 px |
| `hero-decoration.jpg` | Small oval near the flowers | Portrait 3:4 | 600 × 800 px |

- JPG or WebP, under ~400 KB each (compress at squoosh.app).
- Faces near the upper third crop best.
- Different filename? Change it in `src/config/photos.js` → `hero`.
